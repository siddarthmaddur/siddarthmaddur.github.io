03 / RESEARCHMission: early detection

My flagship project, from the research paper to a live prototype you can try.

AI-Based Early Detection of Alzheimer's Using Speech and Language Biomarkers

Young Researchers Institute, Sep to Dec 2025, with technical consultation from a PhD mentor in engineering and machine learning. I independently completed the literature review, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, feature extraction, modelling, evaluation, paper writing, and deployment.

Live prototype: CogniScan

CogniScan uses an XGBoost model trained on acoustic and linguistic biomarkers to screen for early cognitive decline from a short speech sample. It returns a risk probability along with the biomarkers that drove the prediction.
